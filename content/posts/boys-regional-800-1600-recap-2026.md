Same weekend, same weather, same stakes — and the boys brought the heat.

While Region 3 was still drying out from Friday's downpour, the rest of the state's distance runners got their business done. By Sunday, 96 boys had punched their tickets to Myers Stadium. Here's the full picture on the 800m and 1600m.

*The top two finishers per region earn automatic berths (marked **x**). The fastest third-place time across all four regions in each event earns the wild card (marked **x** as well). Third-place finishers who don't claim the wild card go home.*

## Names to Watch in Austin

**Caden Leonard** (Carroll, 6A 1600m) — A 3:59.54 season best. Ran 4:05 at regionals. He's saving something for Myers Stadium, and the question isn't whether he wins — it's by how much.

**Colby Huntress** (Austin Anderson, 5A 800m/1600m) — Doubled in both events at regionals and won both. He is the top returner in both events from a year ago, having finished second in the 800m and fourth in the 1600m and is the current 5A XC champion. His 4:10.69 personal best leads the 5A field.

**Jaxon Asbill** (Dayton, 5A 1600m) — Wild card earner and one of the most compelling stories in 5A. After his runner-up finish at the UIL State Cross Country Championships last fall, he's been building toward exactly this kind of championship run. A 4:11.84 personal best and 4:13.74 at regionals says he belongs.

**Nicholas Ferguson** (Houston Heights, 6A 800m) — His 1:50.64 personal best is the fastest in the 6A field. He ran 1:50.79 at regionals with more in reserve. If he runs his race in Austin, he's a gold medal contender.

**Ray Ramirez** (La Porte, 5A 800m) — Fastest PR of the qualifiers (1:50.84) but needed the wild card to squeak into the State Meet.

**Round Rock** (6A 1600m) — Esparza and Brasil both advance and both carry sub-4:14 season bests. The Dragons could put two runners in the 6A 1600m final and make some noise.

The UIL 5A and 6A State Meet takes place May 15-16 at Myers Stadium in Austin.
